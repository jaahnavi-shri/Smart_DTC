    {# ---------- Why Smart DTC ---------- #}
    {{ icon('bulb') }} Our mission

    Make every bus journey in Delhi-NCR predictable, fair and less crowded.

    Buses in Delhi-NCR run on fixed timetables. A commuter at a stop cannot see how full the next bus is or when it will arrive, and the depot cannot see how many people are waiting. Smart DTC connects both sides with live data.

    {{ icon('alert') }} The problem

    • No live information. Commuters cannot see how full a bus is, where it is or whether it is late, so everyone crowds the door of every bus.
    • Priority seats are missed. Seats for senior citizens, women and differently-abled passengers are often not available to them at peak hours.
    • Depots react late. Managers have no live picture of waiting crowds, so relief buses come late or not at all.

    {{ icon('bulb') }} Our solution

    • {{ icon('check') }}Live seats and arrival countdowns for every bus
    • {{ icon('check') }}Live tracking stop by stop, with "running late" alerts
    • {{ icon('check') }}Digital tickets with automatic PRIORITY ACCESS
    • {{ icon('check') }}Surge alert at 35 waiting, and one click sends a relief bus

    {{ icon('route') }} How it works

    1. 1A commuter checks live seats and tracks the bus
    2. 2Books a seat; priority is detected automatically
    3. 3The depot sees demand: 35+ waiting means SURGE
    4. 4A relief bus is sent and the crowd drops by 35
